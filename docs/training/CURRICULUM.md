# Accounting Training curriculum

Version 2026.11.28.1: 35 distinct source-backed lessons in 13 modules.

| Module | Lesson | Assigned roles |
| --- | --- | --- |

| A — Find your work and trust the right evidence | Find a saved receipt before trying again | Cashier, Front Desk, Accounting, Manager, Auditor, Owner |

| B — Money received | Post the approved accounting entry with a receipt | Accounting, Manager, Owner |
